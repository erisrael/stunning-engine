Seed Town

Welcome to Seed Town

The Myseed 3D world on your PC's graphics card: the city, the Grand Prix, archery, Seed Strike, your home and your colleagues. Sign in once with your Myseed account; your coins, homes, cars and progress are the same as on the website.

Graphics

Presets set everything at once; change any value to make a custom set. Higher values use more of your graphics card.

Display

F11 switches full screen in the game at any time.

Diagnostics

What Seed Town sees of this PC (shared with support only if you send it).

Guest arcade

Seed Town and the arcade games for visitors without an account, full screen in a locked window: they cannot leave it, open anything else, reload or close it. Staff leave with Ctrl+Shift+Q and the PIN below. For a full lock (the Windows key, Alt+Tab) run Seed Town under Windows' Assigned Access.

Account and updates

Seed Town uses your Myseed sign-in (kept on this PC like a browser does). Updates download in the background and install when you close the app.
